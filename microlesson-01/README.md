**Learning objective:** By the end of this lesson, students will be able to break down real-world finance analysis workflows into subtasks and identify where GenAI can add value.

## Why it matters

Not every step in financial forecasting is suitable for GenAI. Some tasks require strict calculations or deep domain expertise. But others—like summarizing assumptions, projecting trends, or drafting narratives—can be enhanced with GenAI’s natural language capabilities.

---

## 1. Where GenAI fits in finance

Here are common tasks in financial forecasting and planning:

- Gathering historical data
- Cleaning and formatting spreadsheets
- Spotting trends in revenues or expenses
- Making projections based on past behavior
- Simulating “what-if” market scenarios
- Creating slide content or executive summaries

Let’s ask: which of these require strict math vs. which benefit from language modeling and summarization?

> 💡 Use GenAI where pattern recognition or communication is the goal—not precise computation.

---

## 2. Case study: CFO with a board deadline

Imagine you’re supporting a CFO who needs next-quarter projections by tomorrow. The workflow might look like this:

1. Review historical data (8 quarters)
2. Identify revenue trends
3. Flag seasonal anomalies
4. Draft baseline forecast
5. Adjust for known risks
6. Generate summary slides for leadership

Let’s break this down.

| Step | GenAI-Suited? | Why / Why Not |
|------|----------------|----------------|
| 1. Review historical data | ❌ | Needs accurate parsing and context |
| 2. Identify revenue trends | ✅ | GenAI can summarize patterns |
| 3. Flag seasonal anomalies | ✅ | Can point out outliers if prompted well |
| 4. Draft baseline forecast | ✅ | Good for directional estimates |
| 5. Adjust for known risks | ✅ | Can test different scenario assumptions |
| 6. Generate summary slides | ✅ | Excellent for narrative + visuals |

---

## 3. Workflow breakdown

Choose a financial task you’re familiar with (e.g., budget planning, expense control, pricing strategy).

1. Break it into at least 4 steps.
2. Label each as:
   - **AI-suited** (GenAI can help)
   - **Human-only** (needs judgment or tools outside GenAI)
3. Pick one **AI-suited** step and draft a sample GenAI prompt.

> 🧠 Sample prompt: 
> *“Given this quarterly revenue table, summarize recent trends and note any sharp changes in the last 2 quarters.”*
